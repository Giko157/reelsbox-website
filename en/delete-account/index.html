ReelsBox AI: delete your account

You only need an account in ReelsBox for the AI features (“ReelsBox AI”). There is no account for sharing, saving and searching.

How to delete your account in the app

  1. Open ReelsBox and tap Profile at the bottom.
  2. Scroll to the ReelsBox AI card.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

You are then signed out on all your phones.

Without the app

Don't have the app any more? Email support@reelsbox.de from the address you signed in with, using the subject “Delete account”. We will delete your account within 30 days and let you know.

What is deleted

  • your email address and all sign-ins on your phones,
  • AI answers that are still on our server.

What stays

  • Your folders and items on your phone. They are only stored with you and stay until you delete them yourself (Profile → “Delete all data”) or uninstall the app.
  • Counters without an email address: So that free requests can't be used again by deleting and signing in again, we only keep how many requests were used for an account number and a random phone identifier. We delete these counters 24 months after they were last used.
  • At Anthropic (Claude): Anything the AI has already processed is usually deleted by Anthropic after 30 days.

More details are in the privacy policy.
