2. In short

  • Your folders and items are kept on your phone. You don't need an account to share, save and search.
  • There is no advertising and there are no analytics or tracking tools.
  • AI is optional. Only if you sign in to “ReelsBox AI” with your email address do we send items to the AI Claude by Anthropic. This involves processing data in the USA (section 8).
  • We only receive content from the app in these cases: when you send a folder (section 5), when you use the AI (sections 7 and 8) and when you write to us (section 11). What Google or Apple pass on to us is described in section 12.

3. What data does the app process on your phone?

When you share content to ReelsBox or create it in the app, the app stores on your device:

  • links (e.g. to videos on TikTok, YouTube, Instagram or Facebook) and their preview (title, channel name, preview image, description),
  • folders, titles, notes and search words you enter,
  • files you share (e.g. PDF, photos, Word, Excel or PowerPoint files),
  • images you choose from your gallery yourself,
  • voice notes you record and the text recognised from them (section 6),
  • if you use the AI: the AI's answers (folder suggestion, short summary, keywords, text in the preview image).

The data is kept in the app's protected storage. Other apps cannot access it. Uninstalling the app or choosing “Delete all data” in the app deletes it.

Videos are never downloaded, only saved as links.

4. When does the app connect to the internet?

For preview images and titles and to open links, the app connects to these providers:

  • TikTok: The app queries TikTok's public preview service (oEmbed) with the shared link. At the same time it loads the shared video's public page to read the title, channel name and preview image. For short links (e.g. vm.tiktok.com) it also checks which video the link leads to, so the app can recognise videos you already saved. Provider: TikTok Technology Limited, Ireland.
  • YouTube: The app queries YouTube's public preview service (oEmbed) with the shared link. When you play a folder as a playlist, it first checks there which videos are still available. Provider: Google Ireland Limited, Ireland.
  • Other websites: The app loads the shared web page to read its title and preview image.
  • Preview images are loaded from these providers' servers or from the server the web page names for them.
  • Facebook and Instagram: The app loads the public embed view of the shared post (as it is embedded on websites) from instagram.com or facebook.com in an invisible browser window. This happens without signing in, without an account and without storing cookies. The app reads the preview image, description and creator name. The preview image is saved as a copy on your phone. Provider: Meta Platforms Ireland Limited, Ireland.

For these requests the provider technically receives your IP address, device information (e.g. user agent) and the requested link. The provider's privacy policy applies:

  • TikTok: https://www.tiktok.com/legal/privacy-policy
  • Google/YouTube: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Meta (Facebook/Instagram): https://www.facebook.com/privacy/policy

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (the feature you use) and Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (our legitimate interest in showing saved links clearly with image and title).

When you open a saved link, it opens in the matching app (e.g. TikTok) or in your browser. That provider's terms apply there.

New version: At most once a day, the app asks our server api.reelsbox.de (Hetzner, section 13) which version of ReelsBox is currently on Google Play or in the App Store. If there is a newer one, the app shows a notice. No content and no identifier of yours is sent, only what every internet connection technically needs (IP address). Our server does not store any of it. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (our legitimate interest in telling you about improvements and bug fixes).

5. Sending a folder

Sending: When you send a folder, ReelsBox uploads it to our server share.reelsbox.de (Hetzner, see section 13). You get a short link and send it yourself with the app of your choice.

  • Always included: folder name, folder icon, the links with titles and their preview images.
  • Only if you tick the matching box: photos and images (photo items, images on notes and voice notes, folder picture), notes and voice notes (the audio file and the text recognised on your phone).
  • For some preview images (e.g. TikTok or YouTube) we only store the provider's web address. When someone opens the folder page, their browser loads these images from there.

Anyone with the link can access everything you included, including the audio and text of voice notes. The page cannot be found via search engines. The folder is deleted automatically after 30 days, or earlier on request (support@reelsbox.de). Your folder in the app stays unchanged.

Receiving a folder: When someone opens the folder page, our server processes technically necessary data (IP address, user agent) to deliver the page. It is not stored. If they tap “Get ReelsBox for free” there, our server remembers their IP address and phone type (iPhone or Android) for at most 1 hour, so that the freshly installed app can offer the folder on first launch. For this the app asks our server once on first launch. On Android we also pass an identifier of the folder to Google Play (install source). If the recipient adds the folder, their app downloads it from our server, including images, audio files and texts.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR.

Older app versions sent folders as a file (a small web page). Nothing went through our servers then.

6. Turning voice notes into text

If you pick a language when recording, ReelsBox turns your voice note into text. The app requests recognition on your phone. It does not send the audio file to the internet.

  • iPhone: We use Apple's speech recognition in “on device only” mode. The first time, your iPhone asks for permission. Apple's text in that prompt is the same for all apps and mentions that speech data may be sent to Apple. ReelsBox only uses recognition on the device.
  • Android: We use Google's on-device speech recognition (Android 13 and later). If the language pack is missing, Android downloads it once from Google.

The app stores the text on your phone and uses it for search. It only goes to the AI if you tick the box (section 8). With “No text” nothing is converted.

7. ReelsBox AI: account with an email code

You need an account for the AI features. You enter your email address and we send you a 6-digit code. There is no password. The code is valid for 10 minutes. You only need the email address for the AI. Everything else works without it.

What we store: your email address, when you signed in and last used the app, a random identifier for your phone (not a manufacturer device number), the type of phone (Android or iPhone) and how many AI requests you have used. We store the code and your sign-in key only as a checksum, not in plain text. An account can be signed in on at most 3 phones at the same time.

Where: on our server api.reelsbox.de at Hetzner (section 13). The email with the code is sent on our behalf by our email provider STRATO GmbH, Otto-Ostrowski-Straße 7, 10249 Berlin, Germany (Art. 28 GDPR).

Protection against abuse: So that nobody requests codes in bulk, our server briefly remembers your IP address and your email address (at most one hour, in memory only). It also remembers until the end of the month which accounts were signed in on a phone (via the random phone identifier, without email).

How long:

  • Your account stays until you delete it. We automatically delete accounts that have not been used for 2 years.
  • So that free requests can't be used again by deleting and signing in again, after deletion we only keep the counters for the account number and phone identifier, without the email address. We delete these counters 24 months after they were last used.

Deleting your account: in the app in your Profile on the “ReelsBox AI” card with the “Delete account” button. Without the app, email us at support@reelsbox.de. Details: https://reelsbox.de/en/delete-account/

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (you use the AI feature) and Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (protection against abuse).

8. ReelsBox AI: processing by Anthropic (Claude)

When something goes to the AI:

  • when you put an item into the “AI sorts” folder. The AI then looks for a matching folder.
  • after you sign in, the app also gradually prepares your older items for search (up to 500 in total). Nothing is moved. The same boxes apply.

What goes to the AI: only one item at a time. Your folder names are included so the AI can find the right folder. Your email address is not sent to Anthropic.

  • Reels and links: title, description, preview image (or its web address) and the type of item.
  • Only with the box “Photos, documents and notes”: photos, the first page of PDFs as an image, notes, your own images that you added to a reel, and your own notes and search words on reels. Without this box, only the title or file name of photos, documents and notes goes to the AI.
  • Only with the box “Voice notes”: the text of your voice notes recognised on your phone. The audio file itself never goes to the AI.

Please don't send the AI health data or other very personal details, for example in notes.

You tick the boxes yourself when signing in; nothing is preselected. You can change them at any time in your profile on the “ReelsBox AI” card.

What comes back: a folder suggestion, a short summary, keywords and the text shown in the preview image. The app stores this on your phone. Our server only keeps the answer until your app collects it, for at most 14 days.

Provider: Anthropic Ireland, Limited, 6th Floor, South Bank House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland. Anthropic processes the data on our behalf (Art. 28 GDPR). It is stored and processed by Anthropic PBC in the USA and possibly in other countries. There is no EU adequacy decision covering Anthropic in the USA. We safeguard the transfer with the European Commission's standard contractual clauses (Art. 46(2)(c) GDPR), which are part of the data processing agreement with Anthropic.

Anthropic does not use the data to train its AI. Anthropic usually deletes inputs and outputs after 30 days. They are only kept longer if the law requires it or if content breaks Anthropic's usage rules (then up to 2 years). Anthropic's privacy policy: https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy

Legal basis: For reels and links Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (you use the AI feature). For photos, documents, notes and voice note texts your consent via the boxes (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). You can withdraw it at any time in your profile. From then on nothing new goes to the AI. Processing that happened before is not affected.

The AI puts the item into a matching folder. You can change this at any time or undo it with “Undo all”. No decision with legal effect is made about you.

9. Permissions

  • Microphone: only for voice notes and only when you tap “Start recording” yourself. The recording stays on your phone unless you send it with a folder.
  • Speech recognition (iPhone): to turn voice notes into text on your phone (section 6).
  • Photos: The app uses your phone's photo picker (Android or iPhone) and only receives the images you select.
  • Share menu: ReelsBox only receives content you share to ReelsBox yourself.

10. Backup file

In your profile you can create a backup. The file is created on your phone. Where you save or send it (e.g. email, cloud storage) is your choice. That service's terms then apply.

11. Messages to us (“Idea or problem? Write to us”)

In your profile you can send us a message. This transmits: your text, optionally your email address or phone number (only if you want a reply), the app version, your phone's system (e.g. “Android API 35” or “iOS 26”) and the selected language. For technical reasons our server also receives your IP address. It is only used briefly in memory to limit abuse (at most a few messages per hour) and is not stored.

  • Server: The message goes to our server api.reelsbox.de at Hetzner (section 13).
  • Forwarding: So that we can read messages quickly, our server forwards them via the messenger Telegram to an internal group of the ReelsBox team. Provider: Telegram FZ-LLC, Dubai, United Arab Emirates. Telegram also processes data outside the EU. There is no EU adequacy decision for the United Arab Emirates. Please therefore do not include sensitive data in your message. Telegram's privacy policy: https://telegram.org/privacy
  • Purpose and legal basis: We use the message to handle your idea or problem and to reply to you if you give contact details (Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR).
  • Retention: We delete messages no later than 12 months after receipt, or earlier on request. Just write to support@reelsbox.de.

12. Google Play and Apple App Store

The app is installed and updated via Google Play (Android) or the Apple App Store (iPhone). Google and Apple process data according to their own policies:

  • Google: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Apple: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

If you have allowed sharing usage and diagnostics or analytics data with app developers in your phone's settings, Google or Apple provide us with aggregated statistics and crash reports. These contain no content from ReelsBox.

If you use the test version via Apple's TestFlight, Apple also gives us the email address you were invited with, plus crash reports and feedback you send yourself in TestFlight. We use this data only to run the test and fix bugs (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR, taking part in the test).

14. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection. You can withdraw consent at any time with effect for the future (boxes in your profile on the “ReelsBox AI” card).

  • You can change or delete your content on the phone yourself at any time (in the app in your Profile with “Delete all data”, or by uninstalling the app).
  • You delete your AI account in the app or by email (section 7).
  • On request we delete sent folders and messages to us earlier.

Objection: Where we rely on our legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR), you can object at any time. Just write to support@reelsbox.de.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. The authority responsible for us is the Unabhängiges Datenschutzzentrum Saarland.

Questions: support@reelsbox.de
